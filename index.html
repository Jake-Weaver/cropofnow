Our mission

Three pillars. One legume.

Crop of Now exists to advance peanuts in every sector where peanuts are relevant, which, after internal review, is all of them.

01

Legume Leadership

The peanut is a legume, not a nut. We do not say this to be pedantic. We say it because it is true, and because it makes almond people uncomfortable.

02

Hydration Responsibility

A peanut uses about 3.2 gallons of water per ounce. An almond uses 28.7. We are not here to name names. The almond knows what it did.

03

Spreadability

Creamy or crunchy, East Coast or West, the peanut delivers. Our research shows it can even become a diamond under enough pressure. Relatable.
