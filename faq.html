What is the requirement for a product to be labeled Peanut Butter?

Glad you asked. By law, a product needs to be at least 90% peanut in order to be called Peanut Butter.

How many gallons of water does it take to produce 1 ounce of peanuts?

We get this one all the time. Peanuts use roughly 3.2 gallons of water to produce 1 ounce of peanuts. Unlike some wasteful nuts (Almonds) which require 28.7 gallons of water per ounce.
