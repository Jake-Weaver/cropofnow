Before these go to print

  • Each print file is 10 × 8 in at 300 dpi (3000 × 2400 px, transparent PNG). Check it against the print area of the crop tee you choose, and resize if needed.
  • The mockups here are flat drawings for review. They are not Printful mockups.
  • Garment colours are suggestions. Confirm them against the blank's colour list, in Printful or with your embroidery/merch friend.
  • Nothing here names a real brand, and nothing jokes about allergies.
  • Source: cropofnow/merch/croptops/. Edit designs.html, then run node render.js.
