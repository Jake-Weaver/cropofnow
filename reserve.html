The portfolio

Four varieties under consideration.

America grows four market types of peanut. The Board intends to have opinions about all of them.

No. 1 · Virginia

The Executive

The largest kernel in the category. Ballpark-grade presence. Tasting notes: confidence, salt, a long finish.

No. 2 · Runner

The Workhorse

The peanut behind most of the peanut butter in America. Uniform, dependable, criminally underappreciated.

No. 3 · Spanish

The Small Cap

Small, red-skinned, high in oil. Punches above its weight. Ideal for peanut brittle and hostile takeovers.

No. 4 · Valencia

The Sweet One

Three or more kernels per shell and a naturally sweet profile. The peanut other peanuts are a little jealous of.
