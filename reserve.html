Why Valencia?
The portfolio

Four varieties. All of them the crop of now.

America grows four market types of peanut. The Board supports every one of them. For our own label, it chose the one at the top.

No. 1 · Virginia

The Executive

The largest kernel in the category. Ballpark-grade presence. Tasting notes: confidence, salt, a long finish.

No. 2 · Runner

The Workhorse

The peanut behind most of the peanut butter in America. Uniform, dependable, criminally underappreciated.

No. 3 · Spanish

The Small Cap

Small, red-skinned, high in oil. Punches above its weight. Ideal for peanut brittle and hostile takeovers.

The Reserve selection No. 4 · Valencia

The Chairman

Three or more kernels per shell, bright red skins and a naturally sweet profile. Less than one percent of the U.S. crop. The peanut other peanuts report to.

Operations · Pottsville, Pennsylvania

Roasted here. In small batches.

The peanuts are grown in the dry, sandy soil of eastern New Mexico. They are roasted in Pottsville. Nobody else is involved in the flavor.

Every Reserve batch is roasted in small runs, to our own recipes, in flavors built around the Valencia's natural sweetness instead of covering it up. Small batches cost more to make. That is the point.
