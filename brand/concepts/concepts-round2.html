Concept 6 · Round 2

The Chairman Mascot

A peanut in a collar and tie, with flat brows and a flat mouth. He is not amused, and he has a 9 a.m. It gives the brand a face for the "memo from management" jokes without borrowing anyone else's mascot (no monocle, no top hat).

Where it lives: Social avatar, stickers, the Leadership page, a tee that's just him.

Concept 7 · Round 2

Peanut O's Wordmark

No separate symbol. Both O's in CROP and NOW are peanuts lying on their side, and the italic "of" carries over from v1. It's the simplest idea here, and it reads instantly on a sign, a box or a sleeve.

Where it lives: Site header, packaging front, storefront banner. The stacked CROP/NOW version is the square icon.

Up and to the Right Growth chart

A quarterly-results line in Field Green that climbs and ends in a peanut. It's the corporate joke in one picture: peanut performance only goes one way.

Where it lives: Investor-relations bits, the Quarterly Results crewneck, the site header, slides.

Concept 9 · Round 2

Grown Underground Botanical

The real peanut story as a badge. The plant flowers above the soil line, then sends down "pegs", and the peanuts grow underground. It's warm, crafted and food-first, which suits the peanut products more than the merch.

Where it lives: Peanut and peanut-butter labels, the tester-set box, the Reserve page, farmers-market signage.

Concept 10 · Round 2

Ballpark Americana

A felt pennant with stitched edges, the kind you bring home from a game. Peanuts are the original ballpark food ("Buy me some peanuts…"), so the brand gets decades of built-in nostalgia.

Where it lives: Merch (a real felt pennant is a cheap, fun product), stickers, the Shop page, summer campaigns.
