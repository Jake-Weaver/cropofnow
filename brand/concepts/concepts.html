The Agency Covert

A field-operations seal: a 16-point compass star behind a peanut roundel, with "Crop of Now / Field Operations" around the ring. It reads like the badge on a jacket you're not supposed to ask about.

Where it lives: Hats, hoodie chest, patches, the "Authorized Personnel" merch line, the site footer.
